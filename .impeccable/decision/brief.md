Scope: operator console (frontend/src) — inbox, new request, request detail with decision. Mode: Operate.

Audience: inside-sales operator and approver; portfolio viewers (technical recruiters and prospective clients, equal weight) who open the mock demo for 1–2 minutes.
Job: scan requests by outcome, open one, understand what was extracted and which rule fired, decide, read the draft.
Constraints: Spanish only; light and dark both first-class; no login implied; no "AI" claims; density preserved; reasoning visible.
Memorable moment: the decision lands as an ink stamp with date and actor over the quote total.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every request is a numbered quote form and every human decision is an ink stamp. Refuses the category-default sidebar + identical cards + badge soup.

OWN-WORLD: Carbonless order-book palette. Light: paper white ground, ink-navy text and top bar, canary and pink copy tints as surface roles (pending = canary, rejected = pink), stamp blue for approval, stamp red for rejection. Dark: carbon-black paper, the same inks lifted. Hairline ruled fields with small uppercase field labels, numbered folio codes (Nº 000123), tabular mono figures. Rank by weight and case inside tables; one reversed ink plate per screen, the pending decision.

STORY: The visitor sees a stack of folios sorted by what needs attention, opens one, reads the customer's own words, sees the extraction and the twelve-stage strip light up the path taken, sees the rule that paused it, and stamps it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ink-navy top bar (wordmark, Bandeja, Nueva solicitud, theme toggle). Inbox: outcome tally strip across the top (five counts, each a filter), then a dense folio table (Nº, cliente, resumen, resultado, motivo, actualizado). Detail: folio header with outcome stamp; left column: customer text, 12-stage strip, quote lines + totals; right column sticky: decision plate (reversed ink) or the applied stamp.

FORM: Carbonless quote book with rubber stamp, position 3 of the ordered list, seed key 767ad4aa. Raises: registered 12-stage strip (botanical folio), weight/case rank + one reversed plate (timetable), full focus on pending with J/K to next pending (vertical feed).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
